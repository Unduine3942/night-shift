# nightshift — Decision Log

One entry per decision that would be expensive to revisit. Record the reasoning,
not just the outcome: six months from now the *why* is the only part that is
hard to reconstruct.

## Template

**Decision:** what was chosen
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Why:** the reasoning, including what was considered and rejected
**Revisit if:** the condition that would change the answer
